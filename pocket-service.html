Process

How Pocket Service Works

Three simple steps to connect with the right professional near you.

01
📲
Open & Locate

Launch the app and allow location access. GPS pinpoints your position on the map automatically.

02
🔍
Search & Compare

Browse or search for the service you need. View profiles, ratings, and distance to make an informed choice.

03
✅
Book & Connect

Place your order or booking directly through the app. Get notified when your provider confirms.

04
💬
Review & Rate

After service completion, share your experience to help the community find the best providers.
